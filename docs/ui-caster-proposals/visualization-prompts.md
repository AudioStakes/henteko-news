# キャスターUI案の静止イメージ生成プロンプト

imagegenの組み込みツールを使用した。
選ばれた[ベース画像](../ui-proposals/images/c.png)を構図の参考とし、各提案のキャラクター、配色、配置を優先して新しい画像を作成した。
画像はアプリの実装や動作検証ではなく、比較のための静止イメージである。

## 01 ペラの まどニュース

保存先：`images/01.png`。

```text
Use case: ui-mockup. STATIC independent smartphone UI design proposal, not an implemented app. The supplied image is a REFERENCE FOR THE SELECTED BASE CONCEPT ONLY: bold flat color, irregular holes and large words. Create a NEW character-led variation. Use the following designer's palette and layout even where they differ from the reference. Do NOT copy reference title/letter C or preserve its colors blindly.
Create a landscape concept board with two equal tall smartphones side by side, both fully visible, ample white top margin OUTSIDE the devices. Put the board label "01  ペラの まどニュース" only in the outside white margin. No persona titles or expert labels inside the app screens.
Palette: #F4E39A butter background, #193F3A dark-green holes, #FFFDF3 cream text and character, #C64B38 vermilion mouth, #2B6280 small blue progress accents. Designer character and staging: Flat cut-paper abstract person: a thin cream rectangular silhouette with the upper-right corner cut diagonally, two small dark eyes, short red mouth and two narrow bookmark-strip arms. NO hair, ears, clothes, tie, robot details or extra facial details. A simple original memorable silhouette. In selection, Pera peeks from a SMALL separate nook beside the question, not from any candidate hole. In reading, Pera holds the upper rim of one big dark-green manuscript window with thin hands, looks down at the text and opens the mouth slightly. A SHORT red line from mouth toward the window connects speaker and manuscript. Keep the body almost absent; text dominates.. Layout: five irregular dark-green candidate holes arranged TOP TWO, CENTER ONE, BOTTOM TWO; right uses one large irregular dark-green text window. Use clear horizontal Japanese sans-serif type and a playful flat picture-book aesthetic. Avoid decorative photorealistic paper texture, deep shadows, old app imagery and unnecessary chrome.
LEFT phone is selection: discreet "おとな", five progress positions and "1/5", the named caster waiting as described, big "だれが？". Exactly FIVE candidate controls all clearly visible and equal in emphasis, in reading order: "ねこ", "いぬ", "うさぎ", "おばけ", "ロボット". Candidate controls contain WORDS ONLY, no individual animal illustrations. The caster must be visually separate from the five controls, never a sixth option. No confirm/next button: one tap advances automatically.
RIGHT phone is the moment this SAME caster is actively READING the completed news. Pose/eyes/mouth make it clear the character is the speaker. Show heading "きみの ニュース" and status "ペラが よんでいるよ". The entire text is visible from the start in FIVE LARGE CHUNKS IN ORDER: "ねこが" / "きのう" / "こうえんで" / "りんごを" / "たべました！". Use actual line breaks and NO slash characters. Below are two generous controls "きく" and "もういちど". All body text comfortably readable; no head or hand covers a word. No giant avatar consuming the full screen, no cut text, extra tabs, ratings, timers, collectibles, microphone-input buttons, hands holding devices or watermarks. Keep the same original character identity on both phones.
```

初回画像のキャスターが一つの候補の縁に乗っていたため、初回画像を参照して次の編集を行い、待つ場所を候補から分離した。

```text
Make one targeted correction to this two-phone concept board. On the LEFT phone ONLY, remove Pera the cream paper-person from the rim of the candidate labeled 'いぬ'. The candidate itself must remain unchanged and fully visible. Place the same character as a small, NONINTERACTIVE header figure immediately to the LEFT of 'だれが？', separated from all five candidate holes. Slightly reduce the question's font size or shift it right ONLY if needed to make this distinct header space, while keeping it large. Pera's mouth should be CLOSED as a short red line, hands in a quiet waiting pose; no talking rays on the left. Do not attach Pera to any candidate or make it a sixth button. Preserve all five candidates, their words and their 2/1/2 layout. Preserve the entire RIGHT phone, the reading pose, all text, controls, palette, board label, phone frames and all other details. This correction prevents the caster from drawing attention to one particular answer.
```
